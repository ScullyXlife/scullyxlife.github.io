Useful around a DayZ community

One place for the Discord work.

Player reports & support

Use private ticket workflows for reports, help requests, appeals, staff follow-up, notes, assignments, and retained transcripts.

Moderation history

Keep warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, reasons, and evidence organized instead of scattering context across staff channels.

Onboarding & roles

Welcome new players, handle starter roles and verification tracking, and publish controlled role buttons or menus.

Announcements & events

Schedule announcements and reminders, run polls and giveaways, collect suggestions, and support regular community activity.

Staff workflows

Configure channels, roles, module access, restrictions, and server-specific policy from the web dashboard.

Community extras

Add leveling, custom replies, temporary voice rooms, starboard features, and other modules only when your server actually needs them.
